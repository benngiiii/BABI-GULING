## 1. Purpose

This document defines the quality standards used to review the learning materials and project documentation developed by Team Babi Guling.

The standards aim to ensure that the AI Overview module is accurate, understandable, well-organized, and suitable for beginners with little or no background in artificial intelligence or programming.

These criteria are used by the Quality Lead and other team members when reviewing chapters, project documents, and the final Stage 1 submission.

The review process focuses on identifying meaningful problems and improving learning quality rather than requiring unnecessary revisions for minor stylistic differences.

---

## 2. Scope of Quality Assurance

The quality standards apply to the following project materials:

1. **Study Guide:** Chapter explanations, examples, diagrams, activities, practice questions, and references.
2. **Project Documentation:** README, Project Plan, Master Task List, and Module Outline.
3. **Learning Resources:** Materials prepared for future PowerPoint slides and video tutorials.
4. **Quality Review Records:** Review reports, revision histories, and review register.
5. **Submission Materials:** GitHub evidence, AI-use documentation, Submission Report, and final submission package.

The standards are applied according to the purpose of each document. For example, a Project Plan is evaluated for completeness and consistency, while a Study Guide chapter is evaluated primarily for educational quality.

## 3. Core Quality Criteria

### 3.1 Content Accuracy

Learning materials must explain AI concepts correctly and avoid significant factual errors.

**Review criteria:**

* Core explanations are factually accurate.
* Examples and calculations are correct.
* Important claims are supported by appropriate references when needed.
* Simplifications are acceptable when they help beginners understand a concept without creating serious misconceptions.
* Statements do not present uncertain or developing technologies as established facts.

**PASS condition:** No significant factual errors or misleading explanations are identified.

### 3.2 Technical Terminology

Technical terms must be used accurately and consistently.

**Review criteria:**

* Important AI terminology is correctly defined.
* Terms are used consistently throughout the chapter.
* Related concepts are distinguished where necessary.
* Technical explanations are appropriate for the intended learning level.
* Unnecessary jargon is avoided or explained.

**PASS condition:** Terminology is sufficiently accurate and consistent for beginner-level learning.

### 3.3 Clarity and Readability

Content should be understandable to learners without previous AI knowledge.

**Review criteria:**

* Explanations use clear and accessible English.
* Complex ideas are introduced gradually.
* Paragraphs and sentences are reasonably concise.
* Examples or analogies help explain difficult concepts.
* Minor grammar or stylistic differences do not interfere with understanding.

**PASS condition:** The material is understandable and suitable for the intended audience.

### 3.4 Organization and Learning Flow

Learning materials should follow a logical sequence.

**Review criteria:**

* Sections have clear headings.
* Topics progress from foundational concepts to more advanced ideas.
* Explanations, examples, and activities appear in appropriate locations.
* Section numbering and internal references are consistent.
* Repetition is limited unless it supports learning.

**PASS condition:** Learners can follow the chapter without significant organizational confusion.

### 3.5 Learning Objectives

Each chapter should support the intended module learning outcomes.

**Review criteria:**

* Learning objectives are stated clearly where appropriate.
* Content supports the stated objectives.
* Examples and activities reinforce relevant concepts.
* Learning outcomes remain consistent with the Module Outline and Project Plan.

**PASS condition:** The material adequately supports its intended learning objectives.

### 3.6 Examples and Learning Activities

Examples and activities should help learners apply what they have learned.

**Review criteria:**

* Examples are relevant to the topic.
* Worked examples are logically explained.
* Calculations and expected results are correct.
* Practice questions assess concepts covered in the material.
* Answers or expected outcomes are provided when appropriate.

**PASS condition:** Examples and activities are meaningful, understandable, and consistent with the explanations.

### 3.7 Visual Materials

Visuals should support learning rather than serve only as decoration.

**Review criteria:**

* Diagrams, figures, and tables are relevant to the content.
* Visuals have appropriate labels or captions.
* Images are readable and placed near related explanations.
* Image files and links function correctly when checked in the repository.
* External visual materials include appropriate attribution or licensing information when required.

**PASS condition:** Required visuals are useful, understandable, and correctly integrated.

### 3.8 References and Citations

Sources should support the reliability of learning materials.

**Review criteria:**

* Important factual claims are supported by credible sources where appropriate.
* References contain enough information to identify the source.
* Citations correspond to the relevant explanations.
* Sources are not misrepresented.
* Links are functional when verified.

**PASS condition:** References are appropriate and sufficiently documented.

A source or link that has not been checked should be marked **Not Verified**, rather than automatically treated as incorrect.

### 3.9 Markdown Formatting

Markdown files should be readable and properly structured.

**Review criteria:**

* Headings use a consistent hierarchy.
* Tables, lists, code blocks, and quotations are formatted correctly.
* Internal links and navigation are consistent with section headings.
* Images and diagrams use appropriate Markdown syntax.
* The rendered document is readable on GitHub.

**PASS condition:** The Markdown is properly structured and does not contain significant formatting problems.

Live GitHub rendering should be checked separately when necessary.

## 4. Quality Assessment Status

Each criterion in a Quality Review Report receives one of the following statuses:

| Status               | Definition                                                                                              | Required Action                           |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| **PASS**             | The criterion meets the agreed quality standard.                                                        | No revision required.                     |
| **MINOR REVISION**   | A small issue or improvement opportunity exists but does not significantly affect accuracy or learning. | Improvement recommended.                  |
| **CHANGES REQUIRED** | A significant problem affects correctness, clarity, completeness, or usability.                         | Revision required before approval.        |
| **NOT VERIFIED**     | Insufficient evidence is available to confirm the criterion.                                            | Verify before claiming completion.        |
| **N/A**              | The criterion does not apply to the reviewed material.                                                  | No action required; explain if necessary. |

### Important Review Principle

A chapter should not automatically fail because of minor wording differences, optional improvements, or reasonable simplifications.

The severity of an issue depends on its impact on accuracy, learner understanding, and assignment requirements.

---

## 5. Overall Review Results

The overall quality review result is determined using the following standards:

| Overall Result                  | Conditions                                                                                           |
| ------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **APPROVED — PASS**             | All applicable mandatory quality criteria are satisfied, with no significant unresolved issues.      |
| **MINOR REVISIONS RECOMMENDED** | The material meets the main quality requirements but contains minor issues that could be improved.   |
| **CHANGES REQUIRED**            | One or more significant issues must be corrected before approval.                                    |
| **REVIEW PENDING**              | The material cannot yet be assessed adequately because essential information or evidence is missing. |

An **APPROVED — PASS** result applies to the reviewed version of the material.

Approval does not automatically verify external links, repository rendering, or files that were not inspected.

## 6. Issue Severity Guidelines

| Severity   | Description                                                                                         | Example                                                            | Expected Action                      |
| ---------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------ |
| **High**   | A major factual error, missing mandatory requirement, or issue that significantly affects learning. | Incorrect explanation of a fundamental AI concept.                 | Must be corrected.                   |
| **Medium** | A meaningful issue that reduces clarity, consistency, or completeness.                              | A learning activity has no usable instructions or expected answer. | Should be corrected before approval. |
| **Low**    | A minor issue with limited impact on understanding.                                                 | Small wording inconsistency or optional clarification.             | Improvement recommended.             |

Reviewers should not create findings solely to fill a report.

If no meaningful issues remain, the material may receive **APPROVED — PASS**.

## 7. Quality Review Procedure

The team follows this review process:

**Step 1 — Identify the Material**

Record the document title, content author, version, review date, and file location.

**Step 2 — Review Against Quality Criteria**

Evaluate the applicable criteria from Section 3.

For learning chapters, focus on content accuracy, technical terminology, clarity, organization, examples, activities, references, visuals, and Markdown formatting.

For project documents, focus on completeness, consistency, accuracy of progress reporting, and assignment requirements.

**Step 3 — Record Findings**

Document meaningful problems with:

* Location of the issue.
* Description of the problem.
* Severity.
* Suggested improvement.

**Step 4 — Communicate Feedback**

Share the review findings with the relevant author or team member.

**Step 5 — Revise and Recheck**

The author makes necessary corrections.

The reviewer checks the revised version and records whether the previous findings have been resolved.

**Step 6 — Record the Final Result**

Update the Quality Review Report and Review Register.

The latest review result should clearly indicate whether the material is approved or still requires changes.

## 8. Quality Review Report Requirements

Each Quality Review Report should contain the following sections:

| Section                                       | Required Information                                                     |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Review Information                            | Review ID, document, author, reviewer, version, date, and overall status |
| 1. Review Scope                               | Material and criteria covered                                            |
| 2. Quality Assessment                         | Evaluation table with applicable criteria and statuses                   |
| 3. Review Findings and Suggested Improvements | Identified issues, severity, and recommended corrections                 |
| 4. Recommended Revisions                      | Summary of necessary improvements                                        |
| 5. Positive Findings                          | Strengths of the reviewed material                                       |
| 6. Feedback Communication                     | Feedback method or communication status                                  |
| 7. Revision and Recheck History               | Previous reviews and current results                                     |
| 8. Final Review Result                        | Overall assessment and approval status                                   |
| 9. Review Evidence                            | Files, sections, or other evidence supporting the review                 |
| 10. Additional Notes                          | Relevant limitations or observations                                     |

### Review Identification

Review IDs follow this format:

`QR-XXX-IYY`

Where:

* `XXX` identifies the reviewed material.
* `YY` identifies the review iteration.

For example:

* `QR-003-I01` — First review of Chapter 3.
* `QR-003-I02` — Second review of Chapter 3.
* `QR-003-I05` — Fifth review of Chapter 3.

The same review series should be maintained when the same material is revised.

## 9. Documentation Quality Standards

Project documentation is reviewed using the following criteria:

| Criterion       | Expected Standard                                                              |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Completeness    | Required information is present.                                               |
| Consistency     | Names, roles, learning outcomes, and progress statuses agree across documents. |
| Accuracy        | Reported progress reflects actual project work.                                |
| Traceability    | Important changes, reviews, and contributions can be connected to evidence.    |
| Readability     | Documents are clearly organized and easy to navigate.                          |
| Version Control | Important revisions are recorded and identifiable.                             |
| Responsibility  | Relevant tasks and deliverables have identifiable owners.                      |

Incomplete work should be reported honestly using appropriate status labels.

A document should not be marked complete solely because a file exists.

## 10. Stage 1 Submission Quality Standards

The final submission is checked separately from individual chapter quality reviews.

### 10.1 Repository and Documentation

* Shared GitHub repository is accessible.
* README provides the required project and team information.
* Project Plan, Master Task List, and Module Outline are available.
* Development statuses reflect actual progress.
* Required project documents are consistent.

### 10.2 Learning Content

* At least one topic is substantively developed in Markdown.
* The developed topic contains clear explanations and a relevant example.
* Useful visual material is included.
* A meaningful learner activity is provided.
* Answers or expected outcomes are explained.
* References are documented appropriately.
* Other chapter development statuses are reported accurately.

### 10.3 Review and Contribution Evidence

* Peer review has been conducted and recorded.
* Quality review findings and revision outcomes are documented.
* GitHub history provides evidence of team contributions.
* Required contribution and coordination evidence is included in the Submission Report.
* AI-use documentation is prepared according to the assignment requirements.

### 10.4 Submission Package

* Submission Report is available in the required format.
* Markdown source files and supporting assets are included.
* Required readable PDF materials are included.
* Required AI-use logs and review evidence are included.
* The ZIP follows the required naming convention.
* The ZIP has been extracted and checked successfully.

The final submission audit records **PASS**, **NEEDS ACTION**, **PENDING**, or **N/A** for each applicable item.

## 12. Final Quality Assurance Principles

The team follows five principles:

1. **Accuracy first:** Important factual and technical errors must be corrected.
2. **Beginner-friendly learning:** Materials should be understandable without advanced background knowledge.
3. **Evidence-based review:** Findings and approvals should reflect what was actually checked.
4. **Proportionate revisions:** Minor issues should not cause unnecessary rewriting.
5. **Transparent progress:** Unfinished or unverified work should be identified honestly.

The purpose of quality assurance is to help the team deliver reliable and useful learning resources while keeping the review process practical and consistent.
