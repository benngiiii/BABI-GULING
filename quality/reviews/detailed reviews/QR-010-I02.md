## 3. Review Findings and Suggested Improvements

The focused recheck did not identify any major content issues requiring further revision.

Several minor observations remain. These are documented for transparency and possible future improvement but are considered acceptable for the current project stage.

### QR-010-I02-F01 — Internal Navigation Naming

**Severity:** Minor
**Status:** Accepted

**Observation:**

Some internal links use generic topic-based anchors, while others use chapter-specific anchors.

Both approaches support navigation, but a consistent naming convention would make the combined document easier to maintain.

**Suggested Improvement:**

Consider using chapter-specific anchor names consistently during later document refinement.

**Review Decision:** Accepted. This observation does not prevent approval.

### QR-010-I02-F02 — Image Path Organization

**Severity:** Minor
**Status:** Accepted

**Observation:**

The reviewed chapters use chapter-specific asset paths for diagrams and illustrations.

This provides a clearer organization than using generic asset references.

**Suggested Improvement:**

Maintain consistent image-folder naming and relative paths when updating or reorganizing the repository.

**Review Decision:** Accepted. The image-reference structure is satisfactory for Stage 1.

### QR-010-I02-F03 — Chapter Reviewer Information

**Severity:** Minor
**Status:** Accepted

**Observation:**

Chapter 4 identifies Brian Djoni Suzanna as both the content author and reviewer.

Although this does not affect the chapter's educational content, separating author and independent reviewer responsibilities could make the review process clearer.

**Suggested Improvement:**

For future revisions, consider recording an additional peer reviewer when available.

**Review Decision:** Accepted. No mandatory correction is required for the current review.

### QR-010-I02-F04 — Figure Numbering

**Severity:** Minor
**Status:** Accepted

**Observation:**

Figure captions are not numbered identically across all reviewed chapters.

For example, Chapter 3 uses chapter-based figure numbers, while Chapter 5 uses a simpler figure-numbering format.

**Suggested Improvement:**

Consider adopting chapter-based numbering throughout the study guide during future formatting improvements.

**Review Decision:** Accepted. The difference does not significantly affect readability.

### QR-010-I02-F05 — Section Heading Styles

**Severity:** Minor
**Status:** Accepted

**Observation:**

Chapter 3 primarily uses numbered sections, while Chapters 4 and 5 organize their content using topic-based headings.

Both approaches are understandable and appropriate for educational material.

**Suggested Improvement:**

A common section-numbering convention may be introduced later if the team wishes to standardize the complete study guide.

**Review Decision:** Accepted. The existing structure is sufficiently clear.

### QR-010-I02-F06 — Minor Wording Differences

**Severity:** Minor
**Status:** Accepted

**Observation:**

The chapters were written by different team members, resulting in small differences in explanation style and technical wording.

These differences are normal in a collaborative project and do not create significant problems with understanding.

**Suggested Improvement:**

Maintain consistent terminology and writing style during future updates where practical.

**Review Decision:** Accepted. No further revision is necessary for Stage 1 approval.
