## 3. Review Findings and Suggested Improvements

### QR-005-I01 — Reference Accessibility and Regulatory Sources

* **Severity:** Low
* **Status:** Resolved
* **Category:** References and Citations

**Finding:**

The previous review recommended improving reference accessibility and ensuring that important regulatory explanations were supported by appropriate sources.

The chapter includes relevant references covering AI bias, privacy, hallucinations, responsible AI, and regulations.

Important sources include:

* Buolamwini and Gebru (2018) for fairness issues in facial analysis.
* Obermeyer et al. (2019) for bias in healthcare algorithms.
* Carlini et al. (2021) for privacy concerns.
* Ji et al. (2023) for AI hallucinations.
* NIST (2023) for responsible AI risk management.
* European Union (2024) for the EU AI Act.
* US Equal Employment Opportunity Commission guidelines for the four-fifths rule.

The chapter also correctly explains that the four-fifths rule is a screening indicator rather than definitive proof of discrimination.

The worked example and learner activity contain accurate calculations and appropriate interpretations.

**Recheck Result:**

The references and citations have been accepted following the reviewer's checks.

The original concern has been resolved.

**Final Status: Resolved**
