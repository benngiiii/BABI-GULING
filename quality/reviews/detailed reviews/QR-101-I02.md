## 1. Review Scope

This review evaluates the revised Team Project Plan and Master Task List (v1.3) against the findings identified in QR-101-I01 and the official Stage 1 assignment requirements for the course **26-27学期-人工智能与计算科学（全英文）2班**.

The purpose of this first recheck is to determine whether the nine findings from the initial review have been adequately addressed.

The review focuses on the following areas:

1. Team information, individual strengths, and skills to develop.
2. Content outline and scope boundaries.
3. Planned learning resources and learning activities.
4. Live mini-lecture proposal.
5. Risk management and mitigation strategies.
6. Master Task List dependencies and task ownership.
7. Task status and document version consistency.
8. Stage 1 submission requirements and package readiness.
9. Document structure, formatting, and navigation.

The review is based on the supplied v1.3 document content and the findings recorded in QR-101-I01.

The assessment distinguishes between the completeness of the planning document and the completion of operational submission tasks.

Repository contents, external links, and the exported PDF's visual layout were not independently inspected during this recheck.

**Status:** Minor Revision Recommended

**Location:** Master Task List; Stage 1 Submission Report; Quality Review Summary

**Issue:**

Task S1-05 is still marked In Progress, with a deadline of October 23 (evening), although the document was updated on October 24.

The task status may be accurate because the recheck was pending when the snapshot was prepared. However, the deadline should reflect the actual recheck schedule.

**Observation 2: S1-12 and S1-13**

The Quality Review Summary records passing results for Chapters 1–5, but S1-12 and S1-13 remain In Progress.

This may be appropriate if other review or correction activities remain open. The task descriptions should reflect the remaining work clearly.

**Observation 3: S1-17**

Task S1-17 indicates that the learning-content PDF has been exported and is awaiting QR-010-I02.

The task may remain In Progress until the PDF recheck is completed. Its status and evidence should be updated once the review result is recorded.

**Observation 4: Submission Report status**

The Stage 1 Submission Report is already included in the document, while S1-15 remains In Progress because the Quality Lead's review is pending.

This is reasonable, but the task should be updated after the review is completed.

**Suggested Improvement:**

Update the relevant task statuses, deadlines, and evidence after the corresponding review activities are completed.

Ensure that the Master Task List and Stage 1 Submission Report reflect the same final information.
