## 3. Review Findings and Suggested Improvements

### Finding 1: Stage 1 Developed Topic Not Yet Finalized

**Severity:** Medium

**Location:** Stage 1 Developed Topic

The document identifies three candidate topics and provides a detailed component check for Chapter 1.

However, the selected topic remains marked:

`[TO CONFIRM by the team after inspecting the files]`

**Suggested Improvement:**

Confirm the team's selected Stage 1 topic and update the document accordingly.

If Chapter 1 is selected, complete the remaining reference, image, and quality-review checks already listed in the document.

### Finding 2: Minor Inconsistencies Between Project Documents

**Severity:** Low

**Location:** Known Gaps and Uncertainties

The document identifies several remaining differences between the Module Outline and other project documents:

* Learning Outcome 2 uses different wording in the README and Master Task List.
* The Project Plan uses "Awaiting revision" while the Module Outline uses "Needs revision."
* Chapter 3 practice-section numbering remains unresolved.

These differences are already acknowledged in the document.

**Suggested Improvement:**

Use consistent learning outcome wording and status terminology across the project documents.

Confirm the final Chapter 3 numbering and update the corresponding references.

The inclusion of Finance and Trading in Chapter 4 does not necessarily require changing Learning Outcome 4, because learning outcomes may summarize major application areas rather than list every topic.

### Finding 3: Chapter 1 Status Requires Final Confirmation

**Severity:** Low

**Location:** Chapter Overview and Stage 1 Developed Topic

Chapter 1 is marked "Drafted," while its supporting table indicates that substantive explanations, activities, answers, and references are already present.

This is not a contradiction because the document defines "Drafted" as content that has not completed the required review.

However, the latest review outcome and verification date remain unconfirmed.

**Suggested Improvement:**

Record the latest Chapter 1 review outcome and update its status once the required verification has been completed.
