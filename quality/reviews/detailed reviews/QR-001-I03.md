### Finding 2 — Spam Filter Score Explanation

**Location:** Section 1.5 — Worked Example
**Priority:** Low
**Status:** Mostly Resolved

**Previous Finding:**

QR-001-I02 recommended clarifying that the spam filter's word scores are not probabilities or confidence measures.

**Recheck Result:**

The updated chapter now includes the following explanation:

> These scores are simple counts taken from the six training emails. They are not probabilities and they do not measure how confident the filter is.

This successfully addresses the original concern.

However, a later sentence in Step 6 states:

> The learning-based filter was uncertain about "free prize at noon" in Step 5.

This wording is inconsistent with the earlier explanation because the model does not calculate uncertainty or confidence.

The prediction is based only on the total score and decision threshold.

**Suggested Improvement:**

Replace the sentence with:

> The learning-based filter predicts "Spam" for "free prize at noon" because its total score is +1, but the prediction may still be unreliable because the model uses only simple word counts from six training emails.

**Expected Result:** The explanation remains consistent with the model's actual scoring method.

---

### Finding 3 — Rule-Based and Learning-Based Comparison

**Location:** Section 1.5 — Step 6
**Priority:** Medium
**Status:** Resolved

**Previous Finding:**

QR-001-I02 recommended adding a direct comparison between a traditional rule-based spam filter and the learning-based approach.

**Recheck Result:**

The updated chapter now includes a rule-based filter using the following rule:

> If the email contains the word "free" or "prize", predict Spam. Otherwise, predict Not spam.

The chapter compares this rule with the learning-based score table using two new emails.

| Email              | Actual Label | Rule-Based Prediction | Learning-Based Prediction |
| ------------------ | ------------ | --------------------- | ------------------------- |
| win money now      | Spam         | Not spam              | Spam                      |
| free lunch at noon | Not spam     | Spam                  | Not spam                  |

The predictions are consistent with the stated rules and score table.

This comparison clearly demonstrates how manually written rules and learned patterns can produce different results.

**Result:** The previous finding has been resolved.
