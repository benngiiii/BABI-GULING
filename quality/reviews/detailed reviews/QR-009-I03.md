## 3. Review Findings and Suggested Improvements

**Severity:** Medium
**Location:** Chapter Overview; Stage 1 Developed Topic; Chapter 1 Section-Level Development Status

**Issue:**

The Module Outline identifies Chapter 1 as Drafted and states that its latest quality review recheck remains to be confirmed.

However, Chapter 1 was previously reviewed through QR-001-I04, which received an approved result.

The Module Outline therefore does not reflect the latest known review outcome.

**Suggested Improvement:**

Confirm that QR-001-I04 applies to the current Chapter 1 file in the repository.

If confirmed:

* Update the latest Chapter 1 review outcome to QR-001-I04 — Approved.
* Update the Chapter Overview status to Checked.
* Record the reviewer and verification date.
* Remove outdated references to a pending Chapter 1 recheck.
* Keep any remaining source or image verification tasks separate from the completed content review.

**Expected Result:** The Module Outline accurately reflects the latest confirmed Chapter 1 quality review status.

**Status:** OPEN — Document update required.

### Finding 4: Minor Cross-Document Terminology Differences Remain

**Severity:** Low
**Location:** Known Gaps and Uncertainties

**Observation:**

The document identifies differences between the Module Outline, Project Plan, README, and Master Task List.

These include:

* Different wording for Learning Outcome 2.
* Different development status labels: "Awaiting revision" and "Needs revision".
* Finance and Trading appearing in Chapter 4 but not being explicitly listed in Learning Outcome 4.

The Module Outline appropriately documents these differences rather than hiding them.

The Finance and Trading difference does not necessarily require a change because a learning outcome may summarize major application areas without listing every chapter topic.

**Suggested Improvement:**

* Align Learning Outcome 2 wording across the relevant documents.
* Choose one development status label and use it consistently.
* Retain Learning Outcome 4 unless the team decides a change is necessary.

**Expected Result:** Project documents use consistent terminology without unnecessary changes to the module's learning outcomes.

**Status:** OPEN — Minor documentation alignment.
