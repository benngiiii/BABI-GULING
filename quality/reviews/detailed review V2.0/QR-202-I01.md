## 3. Review Findings and Suggested Improvements

### QR-202-I01 — Minor Findings

**Finding 1: Estimated Learning Time May Be Underestimated**

The chapter map assigns only six minutes to Section 2.8, which contains twelve practice questions and their explanations.

Although the chapter map totals exactly 60 minutes, learners may need additional time to understand the material and complete the activities, especially if they are studying AI concepts for the first time.

**Suggested Improvement:**

* Reconsider the time allocated to the practice questions.
* Consider increasing the total estimated learning time to approximately 70 minutes if the additional time better reflects actual learner needs.
* Adjust individual section estimates based on the expected reading and activity time.

**Finding 2: Clarify the Meaning of Limited Memory in Modern AI Applications**

Section 2.3.2 includes conversational systems that use earlier messages as context.

This is a reasonable introductory example, but beginners may interpret it as meaning that every conversational system permanently stores previous conversations or learns from them.

**Suggested Improvement:**

Clarify the difference between using earlier messages within the current conversation and retaining information across separate conversations. These are different forms of information use and depend on the system's design.

**Finding 3: Improve the Relationship Diagram in Section 2.5.1**

The diagram is useful for showing that AI contains several classification perspectives. However, its structure may suggest that all categories under "Other Descriptions of AI Systems" are completely separate from the approaches above.

In practice, these concepts can overlap.

**Suggested Improvement:**

* Make the diagram's purpose clearer by labeling it as a simplified conceptual map.
* Show that Generative AI, Multimodal AI, and AI Agents may overlap with Machine Learning and Deep Learning.
* Keep the explanation that one AI system can have several classifications.

**Finding 4: Improve the Step-by-Step Examples for Selected Concepts**

Most examples are short and easy to understand. However, some concepts, particularly Reinforcement Learning and AI Agents, could benefit from a more detailed demonstration.

**Suggested Improvement:**

Add one short step-by-step example showing how an AI system receives information, makes a decision, receives feedback, or performs a sequence of actions.

This would help beginners understand how the concepts work in practice.

**Finding 5: Verify References and External Links**

The chapter includes references from IBM, NIST, Google, and Stanford HAI.

These sources are relevant to the topics, but their actual destination pages, publication details, and accessibility have not been independently verified during this review.

**Suggested Improvement:**

* Check that every reference link opens the intended resource.
* Confirm that the cited source supports the related explanation.
* Maintain consistent reference formatting.
* Check whether publication dates are available before using "n.d.".

**Finding 6: Verify Final Markdown Rendering**

The chapter contains multiple comparison tables and code-block diagrams.

Their readability may change depending on the Markdown renderer and repository layout.

**Suggested Improvement:**

* Preview the chapter in the actual GitHub repository.
* Check table alignment and code-block formatting.
* Confirm that headings and section numbering are consistent.
* Verify any separately stored visual assets and their relative paths.

**Finding 7: Complete Learner-Testing Evidence**

The supplied chapter includes learning activities and answer explanations. However, it does not include evidence that someone other than the author has followed the chapter as a learner.

**Suggested Improvement:**

Ask a non-author team member to study the chapter and record:

* What the learner understood.
* Which concepts or explanations were confusing.
* Whether the activities and answers were understandable.
* What revisions were made based on the feedback.

## 5. Positive Findings

The following strengths were identified:

1. **Clear classification framework:** The chapter explains that AI can be classified from different perspectives rather than presenting all categories as one universal taxonomy.

2. **Good topic coverage:** It introduces the major AI categories and approaches expected in an introductory chapter.

3. **Appropriate treatment of hypothetical AI:** The explanations of AGI, ASI, Theory-of-Mind AI, and Self-Aware AI avoid presenting them as established technologies.

4. **Useful comparison tables:** The tables help beginners distinguish between ANI, AGI, ASI, the four functionality categories, and Machine Learning approaches.

5. **Good connection between AI concepts:** The explanation of AI, Machine Learning, and Deep Learning provides a useful foundation for later technical topics.

6. **Clear distinction between Generative and Discriminative AI:** The chapter explains their different purposes without treating them as identical concepts.

7. **Relevant everyday examples:** Spam filters, music recommendations, chatbots, image generators, and driving systems make the material easier to relate to.

8. **Useful learning activity:** The eight-example classification activity encourages learners to apply more than one AI perspective.

9. **Practice questions include explanations:** The answer section supports learning rather than providing answer letters alone.

10. **Appropriate discussion of limitations:** The chapter reminds readers that convincing AI outputs do not automatically prove accuracy, consciousness, or general intelligence.
