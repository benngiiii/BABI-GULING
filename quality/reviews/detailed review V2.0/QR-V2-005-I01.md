## 3. Review Findings and Suggested Improvements

### QR-V2-005-I01 — Positive Review Findings

**Finding 1: Clear introduction to connectionist AI**

The chapter explains how artificial neural networks use interconnected units and adjustable weights to learn patterns. The comparison with symbolic AI also helps learners understand the differences between the two approaches.

**Finding 2: Effective explanation of artificial neurons**

The single-neuron example breaks down inputs, weights, bias, and activation into manageable steps. The numerical example allows beginners to understand how a neuron produces an output.

**Finding 3: Good coverage of activation functions**

The step, sigmoid, and ReLU functions are introduced with clear explanations, output ranges, and examples. This gives learners a useful foundation for understanding neural network calculations.

**Finding 4: Clear explanation of network learning**

The forward pass, loss, backpropagation, weight update, and repetition process are presented in a logical order. The relay-team analogy also helps explain the general idea of backpropagation.

**Finding 5: Useful comparison of network designs**

The CNN, RNN/LSTM, and transformer sections explain the main differences between popular network architectures and the types of data they are commonly used for.

**Finding 6: Comprehensive introduction to Big Data**

The 5 Vs provide a clear framework for understanding Big Data. The pipeline section also explains how data moves from collection and storage to processing, analysis, and visualisation.

**Finding 7: Practical data analysis activities**

Mission 2 gives learners an opportunity to identify suspicious values, handle missing data, calculate averages, and select an appropriate chart.

**Finding 8: Complete learning support materials**

The chapter includes two practical missions, common misconceptions, quick-check questions, an answer key, a glossary, and references. These materials support independent learning and revision.
