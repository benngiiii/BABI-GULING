## 3. Review Findings and Suggested Improvements

### QR-201_I02 — Remaining Findings

#### Finding 1: Previous Learning-Outcome Inconsistency — Addressed

The previous review identified a possible inconsistency between a learning outcome referring to five key historical moments and the timeline presenting nine events.

In the current version, the learning outcomes no longer contain that inconsistent statement. The history section presents nine key events, and Activity 1.2 uses five selected events for its ordering exercise.

**Result:** Addressed in the current version.

#### Finding 2: Estimated Chapter Time — Addressed

The previous review identified a mismatch between the estimated chapter duration and the total time in the chapter map.

The current version states approximately 2 hours and 35 minutes.

The chapter map totals:

20 + 20 + 25 + 15 + 45 + 25 + 5 = 155 minutes.

155 minutes equals 2 hours and 35 minutes.

**Result:** Addressed. The estimated time now matches the chapter map.

#### Finding 3: Verify Visual Assets and Markdown Rendering

The chapter includes several visual assets:

* `fig-1-2-timeline.png`
* `turing-aged-16.jpg`
* `deep-blue-chm.jpg`
* `fig-1-3-traditional-vs-ml.png`
* `fig-1-4b-training-vs-inference.png`
* `fig-1-4-ai-ml-dl.png`

The image references are present in the Markdown. However, the actual image files and their rendering on GitHub have not been independently checked.

**Suggested improvement:**

* Confirm that every image exists in the correct `assets/` folder.
* Check that all relative image paths work on GitHub.
* Confirm that images are readable and properly positioned in the rendered document.
* Check that image credits and licenses are correctly recorded.

#### Finding 4: Historical References and External Sources Need Verification

The chapter includes ten references covering important AI developments and foundational research.

The references are relevant to the topics discussed. However, the original publications, DOI links, and image-source pages have not been independently opened and checked during this review.

**Suggested improvement:**

* Verify that each DOI or external link leads to the intended source.
* Confirm that the historical claims match the cited publications.
* Check the image-source and license information for Figures 1.2 and 1.3.

#### Finding 5: Learner Testing Evidence Is Still Required

The chapter provides activities, model answers, and self-check questions. However, these materials alone do not demonstrate that an actual learner has followed the chapter.

Stage 2 requires someone other than the author to use the material as a learner and provide feedback.

**Suggested improvement:**

Ask a teammate who did not write Chapter 1 to follow at least one complete learning section or activity. Record:

1. What the learner understood.
2. Which explanation or instruction was confusing.
3. Whether the activity and answer section were helpful.
4. What changes were made based on the feedback.

This evidence should be recorded separately in the project review or learner-testing records.

#### Finding 6: Minor Clarification of the Traditional Programming and Machine Learning Comparison

The comparison table explains that traditional programming uses rules written by programmers, while machine learning finds patterns from examples.

This is appropriate for beginners. However, the wording could be interpreted as meaning that machine learning completely removes human-designed rules or decisions.

In practice, people still choose the data, learning algorithm, model structure, and decision criteria.

**Suggested improvement:**

Consider adding one short clarification:

"Although the model learns patterns from data, people still design the learning process, choose the data, and decide how the model will be evaluated and used."

This would make the comparison more balanced without making the explanation unnecessarily difficult.

#### Finding 7: Clarify the Scope of the Spam-Filter Example

The spam-filter example is one of the strongest parts of the chapter. It clearly demonstrates how training data affects a model's predictions.

The chapter already explains that the score is not a probability and that the method is a simplified teaching example.

One additional clarification would help learners understand that the model's score table is a manually calculated teaching model, rather than a description of how all real spam filters work.

**Suggested improvement:**

Keep the existing note about real spam filters using more advanced methods. No major restructuring is needed.

## 5. Positive Findings

The current version demonstrates substantial development and improvement.

**1. Previous findings have been addressed.**

The learning-outcome inconsistency identified in the previous review is no longer present. The estimated chapter duration also matches the chapter map.

**2. Strong beginner-friendly introduction.**

The chapter begins with familiar examples, such as spam emails, face unlock, video recommendations, and translation. These examples help learners connect AI concepts with everyday experiences.

**3. Logical learning progression.**

The content moves from the definition and history of AI to traditional programming, machine learning, terminology, and a complete worked example.

**4. Strong worked example.**

The spam-filter example is particularly effective because learners can follow the calculations themselves. It also demonstrates that different training data can change a model's predictions.

**5. Meaningful learning activities.**

The activities require learners to do more than memorize definitions. They must predict outcomes, identify patterns, calculate scores, classify emails, and explain limitations.

**6. Good answer explanations.**

The answers explain why a result is correct instead of only providing the final answer. This supports independent learning.

**7. Useful connection to later chapters.**

The chapter introduces concepts that are developed further in Chapters 2 and 3. The shared spam-filter example also provides a clear connection to Chapter 5.

**8. Appropriate explanation of limitations.**

The chapter explains that AI systems can make mistakes and that model performance depends on the training data. This provides a useful foundation for learning about AI ethics and limitations.
