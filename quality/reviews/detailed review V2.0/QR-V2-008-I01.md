## 3. Review Findings and Suggested Improvements

### QR-V2-008-I01 — Positive Review Findings

**Finding 1: Clear explanation of AI ethics and its major themes**

The chapter introduces AI ethics through five understandable themes: fairness, privacy, transparency and accountability, reliability, and safety and misuse. This gives beginners a clear overview before moving into individual topics.

**Finding 2: Practical explanation of bias and fairness**

Section 8.2 explains how bias can enter different stages of an AI system, from real-world conditions and data collection to model design and deployment. The hiring example and selection-rate calculations help learners understand how fairness can be investigated.

**Finding 3: Useful numerical exercises**

The four-fifths rule and impact-ratio examples provide practical opportunities to apply mathematical calculations to AI-related situations. The worked examples and answer key support independent learning.

**Finding 4: Good coverage of privacy and accountability**

Section 8.3 introduces consent, purpose limitation, data minimisation, security and individual rights. Section 8.4 further explains transparency, explainability and accountability through understandable examples.

**Finding 5: Strong connection between reliability and responsible AI use**

The explanation of hallucination and the four-step claim-verification method gives learners a practical way to evaluate AI-generated information rather than accepting answers based only on confident language.

**Finding 6: Relevant introduction to AI governance frameworks**

Section 8.7 introduces the four EU AI Act risk levels and the four functions of the NIST AI Risk Management Framework. The simplified tables make these frameworks easier for beginners to understand.

**Finding 7: Effective learning activities**

Mission 1 develops numerical and critical-thinking skills through a hiring-tool audit. Mission 2 encourages learners to identify unsupported claims and practise checking sources. Both activities include clear goals, preparation, tasks, observation, reflection and feedback.

**Finding 8: Strong connection with the rest of the module**

The chapter connects with earlier discussions of data quality, hallucination, black-box models, reward hacking and human oversight. It also provides a suitable conclusion to the AI Overview module by focusing on responsible AI use.
