## 3. Review Findings and Suggested Improvements

### QR-V2-004-I01 — Positive Review Findings

**Finding 1: Clear introduction to symbolic AI**

The chapter introduces symbolic AI through an everyday instruction-manual analogy. This makes the central idea of using human-written knowledge and rules easier for beginners to understand.

**Finding 2: Good explanation of logical reasoning**

The explanations of AND, OR, NOT, IF-THEN, truth tables, and modus ponens provide a useful foundation for understanding how rule-based systems reach conclusions.

**Finding 3: Practical explanation of knowledge representation**

The chapter introduces rules, semantic networks, and frames with simple examples. The semantic network illustration also helps learners understand relationships between concepts and inheritance.

**Finding 4: Strong expert-system example**

The plant-care example demonstrates how a knowledge base and inference engine work together. The forward-chaining table makes the reasoning process easier to follow step by step.

**Finding 5: Useful search and minimax explanations**

The BFS, DFS, and minimax sections explain important symbolic AI techniques using visual examples and manageable calculations.

**Finding 6: Interactive learning activities**

The two missions allow learners to practise forward chaining, search orders, and minimax rather than only reading theoretical explanations.

**Finding 7: Complete supporting learning materials**

The chapter includes learning outcomes, quick-check questions, an answer key, a glossary, and references. These elements support independent study and revision.
