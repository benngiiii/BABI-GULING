## 3. Review Findings and Suggested Improvements

### QR-V2-009-I01 — Positive Review Findings

**Finding 1: Clear project identity and team information**

Section 1 presents the project name, module, project title, stage and complete team information. The table includes members' names, Chinese names, GitHub usernames, student IDs and assigned roles, making team identification straightforward.

**Finding 2: Well-explained project purpose**

Section 2 explains that the project introduces Artificial Intelligence to beginners with little or no previous knowledge of AI or programming. It also clearly introduces the three main learning resources: Study Guide + Test Bank, PPT Slides and Video Tutorials.

**Finding 3: Clear explanation of Stage 2 development**

The comparison between Stage 1 and Stage 2 highlights the project's development, including the expansion from five to eight chapters, improved learning activities, separate image assets, numbered references and PDF-friendly Markdown formatting.

**Finding 4: Appropriate learning outcomes**

The six learning outcomes cover the main areas of the module, including AI fundamentals, AI classifications, workflows, the three classic approaches, real-world applications and ethical concerns.

The inclusion of symbolic, connectionist and behaviorist AI provides a clear connection between the module objectives and the expanded Stage 2 content.

**Finding 5: Complete overview of the eight chapters**

Section 3 provides a convenient chapter table containing each topic, its main content and its author. This helps readers understand the overall learning progression without opening every chapter individually.

**Finding 6: Clear team responsibilities**

Section 4 describes the responsibilities of the CEO, CIO, Visual Design Lead, Video Lead and Quality Lead. The division of responsibilities helps explain how the team coordinates its work.

**Finding 7: Useful project-document navigation**

Section 5 provides a structured list of important project documents, including the Project Plan, Master Task List, Module Outline, Stage 1 and Stage 2 content, AI-use records and quality review documents.

This makes the README useful as a central starting point for accessing project information.

**Finding 8: Understandable repository structure**

Section 6 presents the repository hierarchy in a readable tree format. The separation between Stage 1 and Stage 2 materials is particularly useful for distinguishing previously submitted content from the current development.

**Finding 9: Clear image and asset organisation**

The image rule explains that each chapter keeps its own assets folder and uses relative image paths. This supports consistent organisation and helps prevent broken image references when files are moved or compiled.

**Finding 10: Well-defined project workflow**

Section 7 explains the seven-step workflow, from research and Markdown drafting to quality review, revisions, resource production and final submission.

The instruction to fix Markdown before producing the other learning resources also reinforces the project's source-of-truth workflow.

**Finding 11: Helpful Stage 2 overview**

Section 8 gives readers a visual introduction to the three-stage schedule and directs them to the Project Plan and Master Task List for more detailed information.

**Finding 12: Practical repository navigation**

Section 9 provides a simple navigation table that helps readers locate the main content, individual chapters, Stage 1 materials, task assignments, quality evidence and AI-use records.
