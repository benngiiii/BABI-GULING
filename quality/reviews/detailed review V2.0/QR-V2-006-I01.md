## 3. Review Findings and Suggested Improvements

### QR-V2-006-I01 — Positive Review Findings

**1. Clear Introduction to Behaviorist AI**

The chapter introduces Behaviorist AI through familiar examples, such as training a puppy. This makes the idea of learning through actions and feedback easier for beginners to understand.

**2. Good Explanation of Reinforcement Learning**

Section 6.2 explains the main components of reinforcement learning, including the agent, environment, state, action, reward, and policy. The video-game example provides a clear connection between the terminology and a familiar situation.

**3. Effective Q-Learning Demonstration**

Section 6.3 presents a step-by-step Q-learning example using a simple corridor. The calculations demonstrate how reward information gradually spreads backwards through the states.

**4. Relevant Real-World Applications**

Section 6.4 introduces applications in games, robotics, control systems, recommendation systems, and language models. These examples help learners understand where reinforcement learning can be used.

**5. Clear Explanation of Behaviour-Based Robotics**

Section 6.5 introduces subsumption architecture through layered robot behaviours. The Avoid, Wander, and Explore example makes the concept easier to visualise.

**6. Strong Discussion of Reward Hacking**

Section 6.6 explains how an AI agent can maximise a reward without achieving the designer's actual intention. The robot vacuum example provides a practical explanation of this problem.

**7. Meaningful Learning Activities**

Missions 1 and 2 encourage learners to apply the concepts rather than only memorise definitions. The activities cover both Q-learning calculations and reward-design scenarios.

**8. Complete Supporting Learning Materials**

The chapter includes common misconceptions, a quick check, an answer key, a glossary, and references. These elements support independent learning and revision.
