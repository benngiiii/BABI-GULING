## 3. Review Findings and Suggested Improvements

### QR-V2-002-I01 — General Review Findings

The review found that Chapter 2 provides a well-organised introduction to the different ways Artificial Intelligence can be classified.

The following positive findings were identified:

**1. Clear Explanation of AI Classification**

The chapter explains why AI can be classified using different perspectives rather than treating all AI systems as identical.

The four perspectives provide a useful framework for understanding different AI systems.

**2. Good Explanation of AI Capability Levels**

The explanations of Artificial Narrow Intelligence (ANI), Artificial General Intelligence (AGI), and Artificial Superintelligence (ASI) help learners understand the differences between existing AI systems and hypothetical future systems.

**3. Effective Explanation of Functionality Types**

The chapter introduces reactive machines, limited-memory AI, theory-of-mind AI, and self-aware AI in an accessible way.

The examples help learners understand the differences between these categories.

**4. Clear Distinction Between Machine Learning and Generative AI**

The comparison between traditional machine learning and generative AI is useful for preventing common misunderstandings.

The chapter also explains how AI, machine learning, deep learning, and generative AI relate to one another.

**5. Useful Real-World Examples**

Examples such as Deep Blue, spam filters, self-driving cars, chatbots, and image generators make abstract concepts easier to understand.

**6. Effective Learning Missions**

Mission 1 allows students to practise classifying AI systems from several perspectives.

Mission 2 encourages critical thinking by asking learners to identify exaggerated or misleading AI claims.

**Final Finding:**

The chapter demonstrates satisfactory content quality, learning organisation, and beginner accessibility. No blocking quality issues were identified.
