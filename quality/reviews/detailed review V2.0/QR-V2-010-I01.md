## 3. Review Findings and Suggested Improvements

### QR-V2-010-I01 — Positive Review Findings

**Finding 1: Clear and relevant module learning outcomes**

The six learning outcomes describe the knowledge and skills learners are expected to develop. They cover AI fundamentals, classifications, workflows, the three classic approaches, applications and ethical concerns.

**Finding 2: Well-organised eight-chapter structure**

The Chapter Overview table provides a clear picture of the module's content, authors and development status. The renumbering explanation also helps readers understand how the original Stage 1 chapters were reorganised for Stage 2.

**Finding 3: Clear development status labels**

The four status labels distinguish completed Stage 1 materials, Stage 2 drafts, chapters under review and approved chapters. This supports project progress tracking and makes the review process easier to understand.

**Finding 4: Consistent standard chapter template**

The V2.0 chapter template establishes a common structure for all eight chapters, including learning outcomes, explanations, missions, misconceptions, quick checks, summaries, answer keys, glossaries and references.

This helps maintain consistency across materials written by different team members.

**Finding 5: Effective beginner-friendly writing principles**

The eight writing principles emphasise plain explanations, short sentences, everyday examples, step-by-step explanations and accessible activities. These principles are appropriate for the module's intended audience.

**Finding 6: Detailed section-level development status**

Each chapter has its own section-level development table. This provides a useful overview of the topics and learning activities included in each chapter, rather than showing only a general completion label.

**Finding 7: Clear mapping to lecturer course slides**

The mapping table connects the lecturer's Symbolic AI, Connectionist AI and Big Data, and Behaviorist AI materials to Chapters 4, 5 and 6. This helps maintain a relationship between the course materials and the team's chapter structure.

**Finding 8: Consistent learning activities**

The planned activities table lists two missions for every chapter. This creates a consistent learning experience and supports the development of practical exercises across the module.

**Finding 9: Appropriate prerequisite definition**

The prerequisite section keeps the entry requirements accessible by requiring only basic computer and internet literacy, without assuming programming knowledge or advanced mathematics.

**Finding 10: Transparent source-verification process**

The Source Verification section clearly records that references have not yet been machine-checked and identifies the responsibilities for opening links, checking source support and replacing broken references.

This is a useful way to document work that remains pending without presenting it as completed.

**Finding 11: Clear documentation of known gaps**

The Known Gaps and Uncertainties table identifies outstanding work involving references, simplified regulatory explanations, learning-resource updates and previous-year project reviews.

Each item includes its potential impact and a planned response.

**Finding 12: Useful revision history**

The revision history records the transition from Stage 1 to Stage 2 and identifies the major changes introduced in V2.0. This supports document tracking and future quality reviews.
