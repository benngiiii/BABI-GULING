## 3. Review Findings and Suggested Improvements

### QR-V2-007-I01 — Positive Review Findings

**1. Clear Introduction to Real-World AI Applications**

Section 7.1 provides a relatable introduction by connecting AI applications to everyday activities, such as face recognition, navigation, video recommendations, and banking alerts.

**2. Good Coverage of Different Industries**

Sections 7.2 to 7.6 introduce AI applications in healthcare, finance, transport, education, entertainment, robotics, and manufacturing. These examples demonstrate the wide range of tasks AI can support.

**3. Effective Comparison of AI Approaches**

The chapter explains how symbolic, connectionist, and behaviorist AI contribute to different applications. It also highlights that real-world systems often combine multiple approaches rather than relying on only one.

**4. Useful Explanation of Driving Automation**

Section 7.4 introduces the six SAE driving automation levels. The comparison helps learners understand the difference between driver assistance and higher levels of vehicle automation.

**5. Strong Human-in-the-Loop Explanation**

Section 7.7 explains why human involvement remains important when AI systems make decisions that may affect people. The discussion of thresholds, random sampling, overrides, and automation bias provides useful practical context.

**6. Practical Framework for Evaluating AI Applications**

Section 7.8 introduces five questions learners can ask before trusting an AI application. This encourages critical thinking rather than accepting AI outputs without consideration.

**7. Meaningful Learning Missions**

Mission 1 allows learners to match applications with suitable AI approaches. Mission 2 provides a numerical exercise involving precision, recall, and workload, helping learners apply concepts from earlier chapters.

**8. Complete Supporting Learning Materials**

The chapter includes common misconceptions, quick-check questions, an answer key, a glossary, and references. These elements support independent learning and revision.
