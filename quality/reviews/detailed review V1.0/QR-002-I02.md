## 3. Review Findings and Suggested Improvements

### Finding 1 — The Relationship Diagram May Misrepresent the Relationship Between ML and Generative AI

**Severity:** Medium
**Location:** Section 2.5.1 — A Simplified Relationship Diagram

**Observation:**

The diagram places “Generative AI” directly below “Machine Learning”:

```text
APPROACH / OUTPUT
       |
Machine Learning
       |
Generative AI
(often uses ML)
```

Although the surrounding explanation correctly says that Generative AI often uses ML and that the two are different concepts, the visual arrangement suggests that Generative AI is a subdivision of Machine Learning.

**Why it matters:**

The diagram is intended to clarify the relationships between four perspectives. Its visual structure should reinforce, rather than contradict, the explanation.

**Suggested improvement:**

Separate the concepts visually and label their relationship explicitly. For example:

```text
                  ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE
                            |
          +-----------------+-----------------+
          |                 |                 |
      CAPABILITY        FUNCTIONALITY     OTHER PERSPECTIVES
          |                 |                 |
     ANI, AGI, ASI     Four commonly      +---+---+
                       taught categories  |       |
                                     Machine    Generative
                                     Learning   AI
                                         \       /
                                          \-----/
                                    Often used together
```

The diagram should be understood as a simplified overview, not a formal taxonomy. The connecting relationship should communicate that many Generative AI systems use Machine Learning without suggesting that the two terms are interchangeable.

### Finding 2 — Some References Need More Specific Destinations and Verification

**Severity:** Medium
**Location:** Section 2.9 — Summary and References

**Observation:**

The Stanford HAI references for Machine Learning and Generative AI both point to a general AI definitions page. The reference titles identify specific topics, but the supplied destinations do not clearly direct readers to the corresponding individual definitions.

The IBM and NIST references also need to be checked against the claims they support.

**Why it matters:**

References should allow beginner readers to find the relevant explanation quickly and verify important claims about AI classifications and terminology.

**Suggested improvement:**

* Use topic-specific Stanford HAI destinations where available.
* Check that each reference supports the claim associated with its in-text citation.
* Keep reference titles and destinations consistent.
* Confirm that the links work in the repository's rendered Markdown.
* Retain the existing sources if they adequately support the material; additional sources are not necessary unless a specific claim remains unsupported.

This finding concerns the specificity and verification of the supplied references. It does not establish that the links are broken or that the cited claims are incorrect.
