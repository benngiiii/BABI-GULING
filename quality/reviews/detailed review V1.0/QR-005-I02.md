### 5.4 Accurate Fairness Calculations

The fairness example in Topic 1.4 was recalculated.

| Group   | Applicants | Shortlisted | Selection Rate | Impact Ratio |
| ------- | ---------: | ----------: | -------------: | -----------: |
| Group A |        100 |          60 |           0.60 |         1.00 |
| Group B |        100 |          30 |           0.30 |         0.50 |

The impact ratio for Group B is 0.50, which is below the 0.80 screening threshold.

The chapter correctly explains that this result is a warning sign rather than proof of discrimination.

### 5.5 Correct Learner Activity Answers

The calculation activity in Topic 1.5 was also checked.

| Group   | Applicants | Shortlisted | Selection Rate | Impact Ratio |

| Group D |         80 |          36 |           0.45 |         1.00 |

The impact ratio for Group C is approximately 0.89.

This is above the 0.80 threshold, so the activity's answer is correct.

### 5.6 Consistent Python Example
