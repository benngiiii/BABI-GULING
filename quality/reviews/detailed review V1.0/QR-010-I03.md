## 1. Review Scope

This review evaluates the Team Project Plan and Master Task List (v1.3) for the Stage 1 assignment of **26-27学期-人工智能与计算科学（全英文）2班**.

QR-101-I03 is the second recheck in the assignment-compliance review sequence. It follows the initial review, QR-101-I01, and the first recheck, QR-101-I02.

The purpose of this review is to confirm that the project plan addresses the identified planning and documentation requirements and provides a clear framework for the team's Stage 1 work.

The review covers the following areas:

1. Team information, individual strengths, and skills to develop.
2. Content outline and scope boundaries.
3. Planned learning resources and learning activities.
4. Live mini-lecture proposal.
5. Risk management and mitigation strategies.
6. Master Task List dependencies and task ownership.
7. Task status and document version consistency.
8. Stage 1 submission requirements and package readiness.
9. Document structure, formatting, and navigation.
10. Quality assurance, revision tracking, and evidence documentation.

The assessment is based on the supplied v1.3 document content and the review findings recorded in QR-101-I01 and QR-101-I02.

The review distinguishes between planning-document completeness and the execution of operational submission activities. The presence of a documented task does not, by itself, establish that the task has been completed.

**Review conclusion: PASS — the assessed planning and documentation criteria are satisfied.**
