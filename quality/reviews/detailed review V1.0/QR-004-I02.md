## 3. Review Findings and Suggested Improvements

### Finding 1: Trading Table Heading

**Status:** MINOR CLARITY IMPROVEMENT

**Location:** Topic 5.2 — Worked Example: A Simple Trading Rule

The example states that signals are evaluated after the day's close and trades are executed at the next day's closing price. The trade entries follow this assumption, although the original column heading, “Trade (at this day's close),” could make the timing less immediately clear to beginners.

**Suggested Improvement:** If further polishing is desired, use a heading such as **“Trade executed at this day's close (from a previous signal)”**.

**Review Result:** The example's calculations and stated timing assumptions are internally consistent. The suggested heading change is optional and does not prevent approval.

**Further Revision Required:** No.
