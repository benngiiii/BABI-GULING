## 3. Review Findings and Suggested Improvements

### QR-010-I01-F01 — Inconsistent Chapter Headings

**Status:** Needs Revision

**Location:** Chapter 4 and Chapter 5 headings

**Finding:**

The first three chapters use numbered titles:

* Chapter 1: Introduction to Artificial Intelligence
* Chapter 2: Types of Artificial Intelligence
* Chapter 3: How Artificial Intelligence Works

However, the remaining chapters use:

* Applications of AI
* AI Ethics and Limitations

Although the topics are correct, the heading format is inconsistent.

**Suggested Improvement:**

Change the headings to:

* `# Chapter 4: Applications of AI`
* `# Chapter 5: AI Ethics and Limitations`

This will make the combined document easier to navigate and more consistent when converted to PDF.

**Review Result:** Needs Revision

**Further Revision Required:** Yes

### QR-010-I01-F02 — Potential Broken Image References

**Status:** Needs Verification

**Location:** Image references throughout Chapters 1–5

**Finding:**

The original chapters contain image references using relative paths such as:

* `assets/fig-1-2-timeline.png`
* `assets/traditional-vs-ml.png`
* `assets/foto-chapter-4.png`
* `assets/bias-occurence-diagram.png`

These paths were originally written for individual chapter files.

When the chapters are combined into `Main/Stage1_Content.md`, the relative paths are interpreted from the combined file's directory.

As a result, some images may fail to appear unless the asset paths are updated or the required images are placed in the correct directory.

**Suggested Improvement:**

Update the image paths to match their actual locations in the repository.

For example, an image stored in Chapter 3's assets directory should reference that directory rather than simply `assets/`.

After updating the paths, check that every image appears correctly in the Markdown preview and exported PDF.

**Review Result:** Needs Verification

**Further Revision Required:** Yes, if any images fail to render

### QR-010-I01-F03 — Potential Table of Contents Link Conflicts

**Status:** Needs Revision

**Location:** Internal navigation links across chapters

**Finding:**

Several chapters use similar section headings, including:

* Learning Outcomes
* Module Map
* Glossary
* References

These headings can generate identical Markdown anchor IDs when combined into one document.

For example, a link pointing to `#learning-outcomes` may navigate to the first matching heading instead of the intended chapter.

This problem may not occur when each chapter is opened separately, but it becomes important in the combined document.

**Suggested Improvement:**

Use unique chapter-specific anchors or update internal links to avoid duplicate destinations.

For example:

* `#chapter-1-learning-outcomes`
* `#chapter-2-learning-outcomes`
* `#chapter-4-learning-outcomes`

Verify that the links navigate to the intended sections.

**Review Result:** Needs Revision

**Further Revision Required:** Yes

### QR-010-I01-F04 — Outdated Chapter Status Information

**Status:** Needs Revision

**Location:** Chapter 1, Chapter 2, and Chapter 3 metadata

**Finding:**

Some chapters still contain draft-related information from earlier development stages.

Examples include:

* Chapter 1 includes a development-status table indicating that some sections need peer review.
* Chapter 2 contains the status "Drafted (V1.0), awaiting peer review."
* Chapter 3 contains the status "Draft (Stage 1)."

The combined document should not present outdated review information without clarification, especially when later quality review reports already exist.

**Suggested Improvement:**

Check each chapter's latest review result and update outdated status information.

If the development-status tables are intended to preserve historical information, label them clearly as historical records rather than current review statuses.

**Review Result:** Needs Revision

**Further Revision Required:** Yes

### QR-010-I01-F05 — Inconsistent Chapter Metadata and Formatting

**Status:** Minor Revision Recommended

**Location:** Chapter introductions and metadata

**Finding:**

The chapters use different approaches to presenting their introductory information.

For example:

* Chapter 1 includes author, reviewer, and audience information.
* Chapter 2 presents its status, author, reviewer, and audience in a single paragraph.
* Chapter 3 includes module, level, study time, and development status.
* Chapters 4 and 5 focus on audience, estimated time, and instructions.

These differences do not prevent readers from understanding the material, but they make the combined document look less consistent.

**Suggested Improvement:**

Standardize the basic chapter information where appropriate.

Recommended fields include:

* Chapter title
* Author
* Reviewer
* Intended audience
* Estimated study time

Preserve chapter-specific information when it is relevant.

**Review Result:** Minor Revision Recommended

**Further Revision Required:** Recommended
