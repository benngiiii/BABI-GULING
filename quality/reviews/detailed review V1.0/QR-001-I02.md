## 3. Review Findings and Suggested Improvements

### Finding 1 — Learning Outcome Inconsistency

**Location:** Learning Outcomes and Section 1.4
**Priority:** Low
**Status:** Open

**Finding:** Learning Outcome 4 mentions eight AI terms, while the terminology table contains nine, including *Label*.

**Suggested Improvement:** Change the learning outcome to nine terms and include *Label* in the list.

### Finding 2 — Model Score Explanation

**Location:** Section 1.5
**Priority:** Medium
**Status:** Open

**Finding:** The spam-filter scores are calculated correctly, but the worked example does not explicitly explain that the scores are not probabilities or direct confidence measurements.

**Suggested Improvement:** Add a short explanation that the scores are simple values calculated from the training examples and are used only for this model's classification rule.

### Finding 3 — Comparison Between Programming Approaches

**Location:** Sections 1.3 and 1.5
**Priority:** Medium
**Status:** Open

**Finding:** Section 1.3 compares traditional programming and machine learning conceptually, but Section 1.5 only demonstrates the learning-based approach.

**Suggested Improvement:** Add a short rule-based spam-filter example and compare it with the existing learning-based filter. This would strengthen the explanation without requiring a major rewrite.

### Finding 4 — Incomplete Image Attribution

**Location:** Section 1.2, Figures 1.2 and 1.3
**Priority:** High
**Status:** Open

**Finding:** Both historical images contain unconfirmed licensing information. Figure 1.3 also lacks confirmed photographer attribution.

**Suggested Improvement:** Verify the original image sources and provide the appropriate creator, source, and license details before final submission.

### Finding 5 — Historical Source Citations

**Location:** Sections 1.2 and 1.7
**Priority:** Low
**Status:** Recommended

**Finding:** The reference list is relevant, but historical events are not consistently connected to their supporting sources.

**Suggested Improvement:** Add brief in-text citations for major events, particularly the Turing Test, Dartmouth workshop, ELIZA, and Transformer architecture.
