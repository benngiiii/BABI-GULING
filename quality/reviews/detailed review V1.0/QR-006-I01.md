## 3. Review Findings and Suggested Improvements

### Finding 1 — Missing Explicit Stage 1 Submission Tasks

**Severity:** Medium
**Status:** Open

**Location:** Stage 1 Tasks, especially S1-15 and S1-16

**Finding:**

S1-15 covers compiling the Stage 1 submission folder and running a final check, while S1-16 covers submission. However, the task list does not explicitly track several required components of the Stage 1 package:

* Preparing the Submission Report.
* Preparing the Markdown source snapshot with its supporting assets.
* Exporting and checking the readable PDF of the Markdown content.
* Collecting and organizing Repository Evidence.
* Confirming that required project and personal AI-use records are prepared according to the assignment rules.
* Packaging the required files into the specified ZIP.
* Extracting the ZIP into a separate folder and checking that the files and assets work.

These requirements should not depend entirely on a general task called “final check.” Otherwise, an important component could be missed without having its own owner or completion status.

**Suggested improvement:**

Add explicit Stage 1 tasks for preparing the report, source snapshot, readable PDF, repository evidence, required logs, ZIP package, and extraction test. Assign each task a responsible person, reviewer where appropriate, deadline, and evidence.

### Finding 2 — Inconsistent Initial Review and Revision Records

**Severity:** Medium
**Status:** Open

**Location:** Document version note, S1-02, S1-03, and Revision History

**Finding:**

The document states that V1.1 was revised in response to `QR-006-I01`. S1-03 also records an initial quality review with the report `QR-006-I01.md` as completed.

However, this report is designated as the initial review `QR-006-I01`. The supplied document therefore refers to the same review ID as both a completed previous review and the review being conducted now.

The report history must distinguish an earlier review from the current review so that the review sequence remains traceable.

**Suggested improvement:**

Confirm which review record actually came first. If `QR-006-I01` is the current initial review, remove or correct claims that the document was revised in response to that same review. If a genuine earlier `QR-006-I01` already exists, preserve that history and assign the new review the correct next ID instead.

Do not change review IDs merely to make the document appear consistent; align them with the actual review history.

### Finding 3 — Stage 2 Chapter Drafts Have Unconfirmed Ownership

**Severity:** Medium
**Status:** Open

**Location:** S2-01 and Deliverable Ownership

**Finding:**

S2-01 assigns the full drafts of Chapters 4 and 5 to “To be assigned.” However, the Deliverable Ownership table already identifies Brian Djoni Suzanna as the preparer of Chapter 4 and Joseph Lokman as the preparer of Chapter 5.

This makes the responsibility for S2-01 unclear, particularly because the task is due on October 30.

**Suggested improvement:**

Confirm the authors responsible for each full chapter draft and update S2-01 with explicit names. If Chapter 4 and Chapter 5 have different authors, split S2-01 into separate tasks so their progress can be tracked independently.

Ensure that the assigned owners agree with the Deliverable Ownership table.

### Finding 4 — Chapter 4 Has No Independent Reviewer

**Severity:** Medium
**Status:** Open

**Location:** Deliverable Ownership — Chapter 4: Applications of AI

**Finding:**

Brian Djoni Suzanna is listed as both the preparer and reviewer of Chapter 4. This conflicts with the table's stated principle of separating the person doing the work from the person checking it.

It also makes the independent quality review of this deliverable unclear.

**Suggested improvement:**

Assign another team member to review Chapter 4. Brian can still coordinate quality assurance and review the other materials, but the Chapter 4 content should receive a review from someone other than its author.

### Finding 5 — Incomplete Evidence Entries

**Severity:** Low
**Status:** Open

**Location:** S1-06 and other Evidence cells

**Finding:**

S1-06 is marked Completed, but its evidence is recorded as `(GitHub Repository )`, without a usable repository reference or a clear description of the evidence.

Some other evidence entries link to folders or files without identifying the specific change that demonstrates the task was completed.

A task marked Completed should have evidence that allows the team to identify and check its outcome.

**Suggested improvement:**

Replace placeholder evidence with the actual relevant file, repository location, commit, pull request, issue, or other supporting record. For S1-06, provide a working repository reference and identify the evidence showing that the required structure and collaboration process were set up.

Where a link is only supplementary, make sure the required evidence is also included in the final submission package.

### Finding 6 — Table of Contents and Table Typo

**Severity:** Low
**Status:** Open

**Location:** Table of Contents and Deliverable Ownership

**Finding:**

The Table of Contents includes “Remaining Stage 1 Priorities” linking to `#remaining-stage-1-priorities`, but no corresponding section appears in the supplied document.

The Deliverable Ownership table also contains the label “Master Task Lis,” which should read “Master Task List.”

**Suggested improvement:**

Either add the missing “Remaining Stage 1 Priorities” section or remove its Table of Contents entry. Correct the typo and check that the Table of Contents links navigate to the intended sections in the rendered Markdown.
