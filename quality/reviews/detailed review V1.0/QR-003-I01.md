## 3. Review Findings and Suggested Improvements

### Finding 1 — Practice Question Formatting and Missing Explanations

**Location:** Section 3.16
**Priority:** Medium
**Status:** Open

**Finding:**

The initial review identified formatting problems in Section 3.16.

Several practice questions appear together without sufficient separation, making them difficult to read.

The answers and explanations are also missing or not displayed clearly in the reviewed Markdown.

This reduces the usefulness of the practice section for independent learning.

**Suggested Improvement:**

Separate each question using consistent Markdown formatting.

Provide a clearly labeled answer and a short explanation for every question.

For example:

**Q1. What is the difference between an algorithm and a model?**

**Answer:** An algorithm is a procedure used to solve a problem or learn from data, while a trained model contains the patterns or parameters learned during training.

Apply the same structure to all twelve practice questions.

**Expected Result:** Students can read each question and understand the reasoning behind its answer.

### Finding 2 — Temperature Table Formatting

**Location:** Section 3.7 — How Modern Language AI Works
**Priority:** Medium
**Status:** Open

**Finding:**

The table comparing low and high temperature settings is combined with the preceding paragraph.

This may prevent the table from displaying correctly in Markdown.

**Suggested Improvement:**

Place the table on separate lines with a blank line before it.

Ensure that the Markdown table contains a proper header, separator row, and consistently formatted columns.

Check the result in GitHub's Markdown preview.

**Expected Result:** The temperature comparison table displays correctly and remains easy to read.

### Finding 3 — Inaccurate Cross-Chapter Reference

**Location:** Section 3.4 — Algorithms and Models
**Priority:** Low
**Status:** Open

**Finding:**

The chapter states that Chapter 2 covers supervised, unsupervised, and reinforcement learning.

However, Chapter 2 primarily discusses capability-based and functionality-based AI classifications.

The reference may therefore mislead readers about where those learning approaches are explained.

**Suggested Improvement:**

Remove the inaccurate reference or replace it with a description that matches the actual Chapter 2 content.

If supervised, unsupervised, and reinforcement learning are explained elsewhere in the module, refer to the correct section.

**Expected Result:** Cross-chapter references accurately reflect the content of the study guide.

### Finding 4 — Training-Based and Inference-Time Improvements

**Location:** Section 3.8 — Training vs. Inference
**Priority:** Medium
**Status:** Open

**Finding:**

The chapter discusses methods for improving AI results without retraining the model.

However, the comparison also includes fine-tuning and training from scratch.

These methods involve additional training and should not be grouped with methods that operate only during inference.

**Suggested Improvement:**

Clearly distinguish between:

* **Inference-time improvements:** Methods that change how an existing model is used without updating its parameters.
* **Training-based improvements:** Methods such as fine-tuning or training from scratch that involve updating model parameters.

The comparison table should reflect this distinction.

**Expected Result:** Readers understand which improvement methods require training and which do not.

### Finding 5 — Image and Diagram Presentation

**Location:** Figures and diagrams throughout Chapter 3
**Priority:** Low
**Status:** Verification Required

**Finding:**

The initial review reported that some figures were presented as hyperlinks rather than embedded images.

Some diagrams also used temporary rendering links.

This may affect image availability and document presentation.

**Suggested Improvement:**

Use standard Markdown image syntax where figures are intended to appear directly in the chapter.

Store important figures in the repository's assets folder when possible.

Verify that the images display correctly in GitHub and in the exported PDF.

**Expected Result:** Figures and diagrams display consistently in the intended learning materials.

### Finding 6 — Technical References and Citations

**Location:** Section 3.18 — Further Reading and Resources
**Priority:** Low
**Status:** Open

**Finding:**

The chapter includes further reading resources, but some technical explanations have limited direct references.

Important concepts such as machine learning, neural networks, transformers, and model evaluation would benefit from clearer source support.

**Suggested Improvement:**

Add a small number of reliable references supporting the main technical explanations.

Use appropriate textbooks, academic publications, or reputable educational resources.

Keep the reference list concise and relevant to the chapter's beginner audience.

**Expected Result:** Important technical explanations are supported by identifiable sources.
