## 3. Review Findings and Suggested Improvements

The second review found no major issues requiring further revision.

The following observations are minor and do not prevent approval.

### Finding 1: Minor Differences in Chapter Formatting

**Severity:** Low

**Location:** Chapters 3–5 — Section Headings and Figure Captions

The chapters use slightly different presentation styles. For example, Chapter 3 uses numbered sections and figures, while Chapters 4 and 5 organize their content mainly through topic-based headings.

These differences are understandable because the chapters were prepared by different team members. They do not significantly affect readability or the learning experience.

**Suggested improvement:**

A more consistent numbering style may be considered during future updates. However, the current formatting is acceptable for Stage 1, and no immediate changes are required.

### Finding 2: Minor Differences in Writing Style

**Severity:** Low

**Location:** Across the Combined Study Guide

Some chapters provide more detailed technical explanations, while others focus more on practical examples and real-world applications.

This variation is reasonable because each chapter covers a different aspect of artificial intelligence. The explanations remain understandable and appropriate for beginner-level learners.

**Suggested improvement:**

The team may improve consistency in writing style during later revisions if necessary. No major rewriting is required.

### Finding 3: Clarify Chapter 4 Reviewer Information

**Severity:** Low

**Location:** Chapter 4 — Review Information

Chapter 4 lists Brian Djoni Suzanna as both the content author and reviewer.

This does not affect the educational quality of the chapter, but identifying an additional peer reviewer could make the review responsibilities clearer.

**Suggested improvement:**

If another team member reviews Chapter 4 in the future, the reviewer information can be updated accordingly.

This is a minor documentation observation and does not prevent approval.

## 4. Recommended Revisions
