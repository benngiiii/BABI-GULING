## 7. How This List Is Maintained

- Jonathan updates statuses after each check-in; each owner confirms their own row.
- A task is ✅ only when its outcome is achieved **and** the evidence is available.
- 📝 means "a draft exists". It is **not** the same as reviewed or approved.
- Every ❓ must be replaced by the owner with a real status.
- Each change is written in the Revision History.

## 8. Project Purpose

This project teaches AI Overview to complete beginners. One shared Markdown content base becomes three connected resources.

| Resource | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| Study Guide + Test Bank | Self-paced reading, practice and self-checking |
| PPT Slides | Visual explanation with diagrams and worked examples |
| Video Tutorials | Narrated explanations and demonstrations |

### Learning Outcomes

- [x] Explain what AI is and how it differs from traditional programming.
- [x] Distinguish AI by capability and by approach, including symbolic, connectionist and behaviorist AI.
- [x] Describe the AI workflow, compare supervised, unsupervised and reinforcement learning, and evaluate a model with simple measures.
- [x] Explain symbolic, connectionist and behaviorist AI and give an example of each.
- [x] Identify applications of AI and the human oversight they need.
- [x] Recognise limitations and ethical concerns, including bias, privacy, transparency, hallucination and regulation.

Each outcome is covered by at least one reviewed chapter (see the Module Outline).
